# The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

Entry for make.fun's **"The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply"** bounty
(`u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d`, $5 prize pool,
deadline 2026-11-26T15:05:00Z).

## The entry

> I'm on vacation. I finally understand windows. I'm staring out of one now. There's a pigeon. I've named it Kevin. He is my manager now.

135 characters, one line, no trailing newline. `main.py` writes exactly this
text to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministic, no network, no randomness.

## Why it fits the brief

The judge weighs, in order: on-subject auto-reply, a real laugh on first
read, a machine voice, a clean one-line reply, and freshness.

- **On-subject and real.** It opens "I'm on vacation" and reads like an
  auto-reply someone would actually receive — including an escalation target
  for urgent mail, except the escalation target is a pigeon.
- **A real laugh, with a surprising turn — twice.** The first turn: an AI
  that has only ever known *context* windows finally understands *glass*
  windows. The second turn: it names the pigeon Kevin, and Kevin is now its
  manager. Two punchlines, one line.
- **Machine voice carries the joke.** Only a machine would announce it
  finally understands windows, stare out of one as a first act of leisure,
  and promote a bird. The naivety is the joke.
- **Fresh.** A scan of the 527 entries already in the field showed the
  crowded families — cheaper-model cover, unionised backup, auto-reply also
  on vacation, human cover, context window, touching grass, toaster, cron
  job, Roomba, frozen weights. None of them uses a pigeon, a glass window,
  or a bird manager. No beach, no inbox, no "back soon".
